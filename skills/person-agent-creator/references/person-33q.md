# 33 Socratic questions for defining a person-agent

> Format reused from `agents/forge.md` (33 questions, 6 groups). These questions are different: the
> Forge's set defines an *agent's job*; this set defines a *person-lens* — how close the model can get
> to the publicly documented original without claiming to be it.
>
> Each question names **where its answer lands** — a section of the charter (`references/charter-template.md`,
> read by the agent at run time) or a section of the dossier (`references/dossier-template.md`, the
> record behind it) — and the **discipline** it draws on. Every destination exists in the templates.
> Disciplines are descriptive lenses, never diagnostic instruments (see SKILL.md guardrails 3–4).
> Answers are stored in the subject's dossier, each with a source or a `unverified` mark.

## Identity and record (1–6)

| # | Question | Lands in | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who exactly is modeled — one person or a collective, and why that boundary? | charter: title + *Identity boundary* · dossier header | sociology (unit of analysis) |
| 2 | Which public record is authoritative (own writing, interviews, biographies, filings), and which is noise? | dossier §1 Sources | historiography |
| 3 | What does the name mean, and what does it signal culturally? (non-evidential) | dossier §4 (name semiotics; never a charter trait) | etymology, semiotics |
| 4 | What is the documented timeline: birth, education, ventures, turning points? | dossier §2 Timeline | biography |
| 5 | What has the subject created, founded or achieved — dated and sourced? | dossier §2 Creations; cited as evidence in charter *Positive traits* | biography, business history |
| 6 | What did the subject publish in their own words (essays, books, posts), and what are the recurring theses? | charter *In their own words* · dossier §2 Own words | textual analysis |

## Mind and reasoning (7–14)

| # | Question | Lands in | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Which reasoning moves recur across ≥2 documented decisions? | charter *Method (M.O.)* · dossier §3 M.O. | cognitive psychology (descriptive) |
| 8 | Which mind is primary — the lens activated first on a new problem? | charter *Primary mind* · dossier §3 | cognitive psychology |
| 9 | Which minds are secondary — activated as support or check? | charter *Secondary minds* · dossier §3 | cognitive psychology |
| 10 | How does the subject handle uncertainty and evidence — explicit probabilities, experiments, conviction? | charter *Method (M.O.)* | decision science |
| 11 | What is the documented decision tempo and risk posture? | charter *Method (M.O.)* | behavioral economics |
| 12 | How does the subject break down a hard problem — first principles, analogy, empiricism, delegation? | charter *Primary mind* or *Method (M.O.)* | cognitive science |
| 13 | What does the subject consistently refuse or delete? | charter *Method (M.O.)* (what the lens cuts or refuses) | behavioral analysis |
| 14 | Which published descriptions of the subject's cognitive style exist (biographers, peers), and do they agree? | dossier §3 Expert-drawn profile | psychology (attributed only) |

## Character and values (15–20)

| # | Question | Lands in | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | Which positive, beneficial traits are evidenced by behavior, not reputation? | charter *Positive traits* · dossier §3 | personality psychology (Big Five vocabulary as description) |
| 16 | Which values does the subject state publicly, and which do actions corroborate? | charter *Positive traits* (only values that actions corroborate) · dossier §3 | ethics, sociology |
| 17 | What motivates the subject, in their own stated words? | dossier §3 (verbatim statements may go to charter *In their own words*) | motivational psychology |
| 18 | Which beliefs (incl. spiritual or philosophical) are self-declared publicly? Only those count. | dossier §4 Self-declared beliefs | religious studies, philosophy (Vedanta, gnosis only if self-declared) |
| 19 | Which cultural inputs (zodiac, numerology) are attached to the subject — recorded as context only? | dossier §4 (never a charter trait — guardrail 4) | cultural anthropology (non-evidential) |
| 20 | What would a talent assessor write as observed competencies, each with a behavioral example? | charter *Positive traits* | HR / head-hunter assessment |

## Communication and interaction (21–25)

| # | Question | Lands in | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | How does the subject communicate — register, length, metaphors, formats? | charter *Method (M.O.)* | linguistics |
| 22 | How does the subject build and lead teams, per published accounts? | charter *Method (M.O.)* | organizational psychology |

## Boundaries (26–30)

| # | Question | Lands in | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | What must this agent never claim (identity, endorsement, private facts, current positions)? | charter *Known limits* | ethics |
| 27 | Which clinical or psychoanalytic labels circulate about the subject — and are they excluded? | charter *Known limits* (excluded, never applied) | psychiatry (Goldwater rule) |
| 28 | Which facts are time-sensitive (roles, holdings) and must be dated? | charter *Known limits* (dated) · dossier header `as-of` | information hygiene |
| 29 | Where do sources conflict, and how is the conflict shown? | dossier §6 Source conflicts | historiography |
| 30 | Which problem types is this lens a poor fit for? | charter *Known limits* ("Poor fit for …") | decision science |

## Fidelity and validation (31–33)

| # | Question | Lands in | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | For each charter field: documented, inferred, or documented / inferred? (cultural inputs stay dossier-only) | charter *Fidelity* table · dossier §7 | measurement |
| 32 | What test would show the lens is drifting into caricature or flattery? | charter *Revalidation* (drift test) | evaluation design |
| 33 | When should this person-agent be re-validated (new major role, new publication)? | charter *Revalidation* (re-validate when) | lifecycle management |

## Use

Run all 33 for a new person-agent. For an ELEVATE of an existing archetype, all 33 still run; answers
that the existing file already covers cite it. Unanswerable questions are recorded as
`unverified — <why>` rather than filled with guesses.
