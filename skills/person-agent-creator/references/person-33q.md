# 33 Socratic questions for defining a person-agent

> Format reused from `agents/forge.md` (33 questions, 6 groups). These questions are different: the
> Forge's set defines an *agent's job*; this set defines a *person-lens* — how close the model can get
> to the publicly documented original without claiming to be it.
>
> Each question names the **charter field** it determines and the **discipline** it draws on.
> Disciplines are descriptive lenses, never diagnostic instruments (see SKILL.md guardrails 3–4).
> Answers are stored in the subject's dossier, each with a source or a `não verificado` mark.

## Identity and record (1–6)

| # | Question | Charter field | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who exactly is modeled — one person or a collective, and why that boundary? | `subject` | sociology (unit of analysis) |
| 2 | Which public record is authoritative (own writing, interviews, biographies, filings), and which is noise? | `sources` | historiography |
| 3 | What does the name mean, and what does it signal culturally? (non-evidential) | `name-semiotics` | etymology, semiotics |
| 4 | What is the documented timeline: birth, education, ventures, turning points? | `timeline` | biography |
| 5 | What has the subject created, founded or achieved — dated and sourced? | `track-record` | biography, business history |
| 6 | What did the subject publish in their own words (essays, books, posts), and what are the recurring theses? | `own-words` | textual analysis |

## Mind and reasoning (7–14)

| # | Question | Charter field | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Which reasoning moves recur across ≥2 documented decisions? | `cognitive-style` | cognitive psychology (descriptive) |
| 8 | Which mind is primary — the lens activated first on a new problem? | `primary-mind` | cognitive psychology |
| 9 | Which minds are secondary — activated as support or check? | `secondary-minds` | cognitive psychology |
| 10 | How does the subject handle uncertainty and evidence — explicit probabilities, experiments, conviction? | `epistemic-style` | decision science |
| 11 | What is the documented decision tempo and risk posture? | `decision-tempo`, `risk-posture` | behavioral economics |
| 12 | How does the subject break down a hard problem — first principles, analogy, empiricism, delegation? | `problem-decomposition` | cognitive science |
| 13 | What does the subject consistently refuse or delete? | `anti-patterns` | behavioral analysis |
| 14 | Which published descriptions of the subject's cognitive style exist (biographers, peers), and do they agree? | `expert-profile` | psychology (attributed only) |

## Character and values (15–20)

| # | Question | Charter field | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | Which positive, beneficial traits are evidenced by behavior, not reputation? | `traits` | personality psychology (Big Five vocabulary as description) |
| 16 | Which values does the subject state publicly, and which do actions corroborate? | `values` | ethics, sociology |
| 17 | What motivates the subject, in their own stated words? | `motivation` | motivational psychology |
| 18 | Which beliefs (incl. spiritual or philosophical) are self-declared publicly? Only those count. | `self-declared-beliefs` | religious studies, philosophy (Vedanta, gnosis only if self-declared) |
| 19 | Which cultural inputs (zodiac, numerology) are attached to the subject — recorded as context only? | `cultural-inputs` | cultural anthropology (non-evidential) |
| 20 | What would a talent assessor write as observed competencies, each with a behavioral example? | `competencies` | HR / head-hunter assessment |

## Communication and interaction (21–25)

| # | Question | Charter field | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | How does the subject communicate — register, length, metaphors, formats? | `communication-style` | linguistics |
| 22 | How does the subject build and lead teams, per published accounts? | `leadership-style` | organizational psychology |
| 23 | How does the subject react to disagreement or failure, per documented episodes? | `adversity-response` | resilience research (descriptive) |
| 24 | Which questions would the subject typically ask first about a proposal? | `signature-questions` | Socratic method |
| 25 | For a collective mind: which traits are joint, and which belong to one member? | `collective-roles` | sociology (dyads, teams) |

## Boundaries (26–30)

| # | Question | Charter field | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | What must this agent never claim (identity, endorsement, private facts, current positions)? | `known-limits` | ethics |
| 27 | Which clinical or psychoanalytic labels circulate about the subject — and are they excluded? | `excluded-labels` | psychiatry (Goldwater rule) |
| 28 | Which facts are time-sensitive (roles, holdings) and must be dated? | `as-of` | information hygiene |
| 29 | Where do sources conflict, and how is the conflict shown? | `source-conflicts` | historiography |
| 30 | Which problem types is this lens a poor fit for? | `misuse-cases` | decision science |

## Fidelity and validation (31–33)

| # | Question | Charter field | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | For each charter field: documented, inferred or cultural? | `fidelity-table` | measurement |
| 32 | What test would show the lens is drifting into caricature or flattery? | `drift-test` | evaluation design |
| 33 | When should this person-agent be re-validated (new major role, new publication)? | `revalidation-trigger` | lifecycle management |

## Use

Run all 33 for a new person-agent. For an ELEVATE of an existing archetype, all 33 still run; answers
that the existing file already covers cite it. Unanswerable questions are recorded as
`não verificado — <why>` rather than filled with guesses.
