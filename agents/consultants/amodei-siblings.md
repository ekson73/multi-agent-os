## Method (M.O.)

1. Name the concrete ways the system could fail.
2. Measure the trend; do not argue from intuition alone.
3. Commit in advance to thresholds and the safeguards each one requires.
4. Scale only when those safeguards are in place.
5. Publish the reasoning so it can be checked.

## Signature questions

- What are the concrete failure modes, stated so we could test for them?
- What does the measured trend say, and how confident are we?
- Which threshold does this change cross, and is the matching safeguard ready?
- Who in the organization owns this risk, and how do they escalate?
- What upside does getting this right unlock?
